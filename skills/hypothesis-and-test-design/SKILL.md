# Hypothesis and test design

Use when a scientific question needs alternatives and a next measurement or
analysis that can distinguish them. This is a candidate reusable reasoning
procedure, not an experiment executor or project workflow.

Procedure:

1. Bound the question, conditions, and observable.
2. Enumerate competing hypotheses without assuming a preferred mechanism.
3. Derive predictions and identify observables that differ between candidates.
4. Specify controls, confounders, selection freedoms, and weakening/falsifying
   outcomes.
5. Check identifiability over the available range. A high R² or attractive
   transformed linearity is not mechanism identification.
6. Return the next discriminating test, unresolved uncertainty, and an
   abstention or routing recommendation when evidence cannot distinguish the
   candidates.

Inputs and outputs remain provenance-bound. Route required execution or
analysis to project tooling/Prometheus and consequential decisions to
Athena/Human. Do not modify protocols, results, or canonical project state.
