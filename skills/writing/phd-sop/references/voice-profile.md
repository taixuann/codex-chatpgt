# Voice profile and provenance

## Provenance classes

- USER_ORIGINAL
- USER_APPROVED
- AI_ASSISTED_APPROVED
- AI_DRAFT_UNAPPROVED
- UNKNOWN

Calibration priority:
1. user-original prose;
2. user edits;
3. explicitly approved sentences/paragraphs;
4. AI-assisted text explicitly approved by the user;
5. unapproved AI drafts only as negative/contrastive material.

Never infer a user's voice from an AI-heavy draft merely because it is the latest draft.

## Initial profile for the current calibration corpus

Positive:
- direct;
- observational;
- restrained;
- evidence-led;
- technically specific when useful;
- reflective after evidence rather than before it;
- modest without defensive hedging;
- first-person agency when ownership matters;
- question-driven;
- concrete verbs;
- low performative enthusiasm.

Risks:
- ornamental academic prose;
- generic admissions language;
- inflated adjectives;
- self-labeling traits;
- manufactured epiphany;
- empty abstraction;
- excessive signposting;
- polished-but-detached wording;
- forced symmetry;
- repeated weak sentence templates.

## Preservation preflight

After a rewrite, verify:
- facts;
- chronology;
- ownership;
- uncertainty/qualifications;
- claim ceiling;
- research identity;
- user-approved quirks and rhythm.

Clarity improvements are allowed. Native-speakerization, personality substitution, and evidence strengthening are not.
