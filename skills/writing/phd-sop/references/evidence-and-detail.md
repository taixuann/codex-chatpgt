# Evidence, detail, and cross-document consistency

## Evidence lock

Every material statement should be traceable to supplied facts, documents, or verified program/PI sources. If the source is vague, keep the claim vague or surface the gap.

## Ownership

Separate:
- idea proposed by supervisor/collaborator;
- applicant execution;
- shared work;
- collaborator-only measurement/acquisition;
- applicant analysis/interpretation.

Do not compress a team project into first-person ownership.

## Cross-document conflicts

When CV, SOP, notes, or application records are supplied, flag material conflicts in:
- role;
- dates/duration;
- instrument/measurement ownership;
- publication status;
- numeric result;
- award wording;
- institution/lab/PI identity.

Use `CROSS_DOCUMENT_CONFLICT`. A material unresolved conflict fails `G08 MATERIAL_CONSISTENCY`.

## Detail

Use concrete detail when it supports evidence, chronology, causality, or applicant agency. Omit or compress technically true details that do not change reader understanding.

Reliability/population-level analysis may be a scientific-maturity signal, but named techniques must not be inserted merely as skill badges.
