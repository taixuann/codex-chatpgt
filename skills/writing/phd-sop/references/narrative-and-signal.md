# Narrative and signal construction

Narrative in this skill means evidence-bounded causal and intellectual movement, not decorative storytelling.

## Ephemeral story map

For material episodes, resolve when supported:
- initial state;
- trigger/prompt;
- applicant action;
- constraint;
- unexpected observation;
- verification;
- next limitation/question;
- downstream action;
- ownership;
- evidence status;
- candidate reader signals.

Missing fields stay unresolved. Do not fill them for symmetry.

## Signal value

Use `KEEP_CENTRAL`, `KEEP_COMPRESSED`, `OMIT_DEFAULT`, or `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`.

Judge:
- document relevance;
- evidence confidence;
- applicant agency;
- distinctiveness;
- consequence for later research;
- reader inference enabled;
- genre necessity;
- representativeness;
- word cost;
- coverage opportunity cost.

Technical specificity is useful only when it supports a document function.

## Causal authenticity

Prefer a supported chain such as:
`constraint/question → action → observation → limitation/consequence → next action/question`.

Never project later understanding backward. A supervisor-suggested experiment cannot become the applicant's original hypothesis merely because that sentence sounds stronger.

Separate:
- **narrative causality**: an event motivated a later decision;
- **scientific causality**: one physical process caused another.

A valid narrative connection does not establish a mechanism.

## Turning points

A turning point needs a supported before state, real event, changed question/method/judgment, and downstream consequence. Prefer cumulative growth when that is what the record shows. Flag manufactured epiphany, retroactive destiny, and drama without consequence.

## Researcher legibility

Allow the reader to infer how the applicant works from evidence. Recurrence across episodes strengthens a pattern but is not mandatory; never force every project into one personality slogan.
