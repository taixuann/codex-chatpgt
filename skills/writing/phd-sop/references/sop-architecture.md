# SOP architecture

Program prompt overrides generic architecture.

A research-heavy SOP should make it possible to identify:
- what the applicant has done;
- what they actually owned;
- how questions/judgment changed;
- what they want to investigate next;
- why doctoral training is needed;
- why the program/PI environment fits when requested.

Architecture is not a mandatory six-paragraph template. Paragraphs need a primary job and should move the document state. Chronology alone is not trajectory.

Drafting order:
1. resolve mode and policy;
2. lock meaning/evidence/ownership;
3. map episodes and narrative signal;
4. compare plausible spines if materially different;
5. assign paragraph jobs;
6. draft in the active voice;
7. preservation and cross-document preflight;
8. review from document level down, then lexical cleanup.

Do not line-edit a structurally unstable draft.
