# Scientific method critique

Use for scientific reasoning about a method or protocol, not for executing or
validating its project artifact lifecycle. Project-local `study-run`,
`protocol-author-review`, DC-IV, temperature-IV, figure, and communication
skills remain the owning procedures.

Required method context: question, method identity/revision, intended output,
assumptions, admissibility conditions, controls, and known alternatives.

Review:

- why the method exists and what question it answers;
- when it is admissible and what selection freedom matters;
- assumptions, controls, confounders, diagnostics, and competing methods/models;
- failure and abstention conditions;
- the maximum claim strength the method can license.

Return bounded findings, limitations, and a discriminating next check. A
missing or stale method contract is a method/provenance gap, not permission to
invent one. Route implementation or execution gaps to Prometheus/project
tooling and consequential method decisions to the human owner.
